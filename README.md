# SkillSphere – Your Skills. Your Opportunities.

SkillSphere is a student-focused platform that connects students with real opportunities to showcase their talents, discover micro-gigs, collaborate on projects, and build professional portfolios.

## Live Website

https://skill-sphere-eco.lovable.app

## Purpose

⭐Our mission is to empower students to transform their skills into practical experience, new opportunities, and career growth.

## Key Features

* Showcase skills and talents
* Discover project opportunities
* Collaborate with others
* Build professional portfolios

**Learn → Do → Earn → Prove → Grow**
